# Product

## Register

product

## Users

DriveAlert serves Drivers who need a fast, glanceable view of device readiness, monitoring sessions, and confirmed warning history, plus Trusted Contacts who review only escalation-eligible records shared by connected Drivers. Drivers may use the interface before or after a trip and should never need to understand computer-vision metrics.

## Product Purpose

DriveAlert is a mobile interface for setting up a drowsiness-warning device, checking monitoring readiness, reviewing locally simulated warning records, and managing Trusted Contact visibility. This phase is a complete offline UI prototype whose success is clear navigation, credible states, and a structure that can later accept real services without redesign.

## Brand Personality

Calm, vigilant, trustworthy. The interface should feel safety-conscious and dependable without making every screen feel like an emergency.

## Anti-references

Avoid an alarm-heavy dashboard: excessive red, constant danger styling, dense warning noise, medical-diagnosis language, and exposed implementation metrics. Avoid decorative glass, neon-on-black styling, and unfamiliar controls that slow a safety-related task.

## Design Principles

- Make readiness and the next safe action obvious at a glance.
- Reserve urgency for confirmed escalation; routine states stay calm.
- Use familiar mobile controls and consistent navigation across roles.
- Explain status in plain language, never with hidden technical measurements.
- Keep mock behavior credible and replaceable by future real services.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG AA-style contrast, 48dp touch targets, scalable text, descriptive semantics, reduced-motion support, and status communication that never relies on color alone.
